Recipe for Fried Rice

This is a simple fried rice dish with vegetables and egg.

This is a dish that is easy to modify for different preferences and dietary needs.

Make it vegan by omitting the egg.

Ingredients

  • White or brown rice (cooked at least a day before and cooled in refrigerator)
  • Vegetables like mushrooms, green peas, carrots, broccoli
  • Optional protein like shrimp, chicken, or tofu
  • Green onion
  • Tamari sauce (or soy sauce if it doesn't need to be gluten free)
  • Egg, if using
  • White wine (optional)
  • Salt, pepper, sesame seeds as needed

Directions

  1. Cook the rice on the stovetop or in a rice cooker and cool in the fridge for at least a day.
  2. Alternatively, can cook the rice and then spread it on a sheet pan and cool it in the fridge for 30-90 minutes.
  3. Put rice in saute pan with a little neutral oil and toast, breaking up clumps of rice as you go.
  4. Add and saute vegetables.
  5. Add tamari or soy sauce--no exact measurements, but I do five swirls.
  6. Add green onion.
  7. Add egg, if using, and cook directly in pan.
  8. Salt and pepper to taste. Sesame seeds to taste, if using.
