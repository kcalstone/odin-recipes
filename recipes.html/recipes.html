Recipe for Risotto

This is a beautiful photo of risotto with greens.

This is a gluten-free and dairy-free risotto, friendly for Celiacs.

Feel free to make any substitutions for your own dietary needs.

Ingredients

  • Olive oil
  • Short-grain rice
  • Mushrooms
  • Garlic
  • Tamari sauce
  • Vegetable or chicken broth
  • White wine (optional)
  • Salt, pepper
  • Lemon juice
  • Any additions like capers or hearts of palm

Directions

  1. Boil several cups of water to have hot and ready.
  2. Toast your rice for 3-4 minutes in a saute pan in olive oil, until light golden.
  3. Add and saute mushrooms until they release some of their liquid. Add splash of tamari and 1 tsp or more of garlic.
  4. Add one glug of white wine, if using.
  5. If not using wine, add about 1/3 cup broth.
  6. Stir continuously and add broth until rice is cooked with only slight crunch.
  7. Add a squirt of lemon juice near the end.
  8. Salt and pepper to taste.

Recipe for Fried Rice

This is a simple fried rice dish with vegetables and egg.

This is a dish that is easy to modify for different preferences and dietary needs.

Make it vegan by omitting the egg.

Ingredients

  • White or brown rice (cooked at least a day before and cooled in refrigerator)
  • Vegetables like mushrooms, green peas, carrots, broccoli
  • Optional protein like shrimp, chicken, or tofu
  • Green onion
  • Tamari sauce (or soy sauce if it doesn't need to be gluten free)
  • Egg, if using
  • White wine (optional)
  • Salt, pepper, sesame seeds as needed

Directions

  1. Cook the rice on the stovetop or in a rice cooker and cool in the fridge for at least a day.
  2. Alternatively, can cook the rice and then spread it on a sheet pan and cool it in the fridge for 30-90 minutes.
  3. Put rice in saute pan with a little neutral oil and toast, breaking up clumps of rice as you go.
  4. Add and saute vegetables.
  5. Add tamari or soy sauce--no exact measurements, but I do five swirls.
  6. Add green onion.
  7. Add egg, if using, and cook directly in pan.
  8. Salt and pepper to taste. Sesame seeds to taste, if using.

Recipe for Simple Chicken Soup

This is a simple chicken soup dish.

This is a dish that is easy to modify for different preferences and dietary needs.

Make it vegan by using vegetable broth and using soaked dry white beans rather than chicken.

Ingredients

  • Chicken or vegetable broth
  • Chicken--can be pre-cooked rotisserie, or boiled or otherwise cooked from raw.
  • Olive oil
  • One onion, 2-3 carrots, 2-3 stalks of celery, all diced
  • Green onion (optional)
  • Herbs like basil, herbes de provence, garlic, bay leaf
  • Celery salt
  • Salt, pepper to taste

Directions

  1. Cook chicken, if not cooked. Can boil or cook any other way you choose (broil, grill, etc.)
  2. If using beans instead of chicken: soak dried beans for 3-12 hours in plain water. Then add beans to broth and prepare to cook for 2-3 hours.
  3. Saute onion, carrot, and celery in olive oil for 4 minutes, or until onions are translucent and all vegetables are softened.
  4. Add chicken (or beans, if using instead.)
  5. Add broth to cover. Add herbs and celery salt.
  6. Bring to boil, then lower to medium low heat and cook for ~45 minutes (for chicken) or 2-3 hours (for beans, until softened and broth is creamier).
  7. Can either add noodles (or rice, for gluten free) directly into broth or cook separately and spoon the soup over it.
  8. Salt and pepper to taste.
